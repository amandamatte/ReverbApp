# 🎧 Reverb

A cozy little corner to explore, review, and share your favorite tunes! ✨

Reverb is a music discovery app where you can rate albums, discover new artists, and build a beautiful profile to showcase your unique music taste. Think of it as a digital diary for your daily soundtrack. 🎵

### 🌟 What's Inside

* **✨ Personalized Magic:** Pick your favorite genres and artists to set up your cozy space.
* **💌 Made for You:** Get recommendations based on the tracks you already have on repeat.
* **✍️ Share Your Thoughts:** Leave star ratings, write reviews, and read what the community is listening to.
* **💖 Your Music Profile:** Keep a log of your favorite songs, top albums, and your overall listening activity.
* **🔍 Discover:** Browse curated genre cards and search for what to review next.

### 🛠️ Built With

* **SwiftUI** for a smooth, native iOS experience
* **Figma** for custom visual assets and UI design

### 🌱 Let's Get Started

Want to run Reverb on your own machine?

1. Open up Xcode.
2. Clone this little repo:
```bash
git clone https://github.com/your_username/reverb.git

```


3. Build and hit play (`Cmd + R`) to run it on your iOS Simulator!
